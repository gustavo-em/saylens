# SayLens — Etiqueta concept

## Idea

Every object in the world gets a label. The tag is the label; the accent disc
inside it is the object.

## Status: not working — recommend dropping

Two rewrites, both failed, and the failure is in the concept rather than the
execution.

- **First version** — horizontal tag, small punched hole, amber. At 29 px the
  taper collapsed and the hole merged with the edge.
- **Second version** — larger, rotated −32° to fill the square's diagonal, hole
  replaced by a solid indigo disc for hierarchy, wedge point sharpened. Bigger
  and better composed, and it still fails: what survives at 29 px is a cream blob
  with a blue dot. The tag silhouette needs its point and its straight edges to
  be read as a tag, and a shape that small cannot hold both.

There is also a category problem that no amount of redrawing fixes: a tag with a
punched hole is price-tag shorthand, so the first read is e-commerce.

The idea underneath — labelling the world, every object acquiring its name — is
the most emotionally accurate of the three concepts. It is worth carrying into a
different form. This form is not the one.
