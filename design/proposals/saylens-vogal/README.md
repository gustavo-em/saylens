# SayLens — Vogal concept

## Idea

The shape a mouth makes on an open vowel, which is also an aperture seen head on.

## Craft

Two rewrites. The first was a flat single-colour ring — legible and completely
inert. The second split it into two arcs with gaps at the corners, which read as
a face (brow over eye) and fell apart at 29 px. What is here now:

- **Two-tone, no gap.** Indigo upper half, cream lower half, meeting flush. One
  accent, one neutral, and the split reads as a single object rather than two.
- **Off-centre counter.** The inner void sits at (486, 476) rather than at the
  centre, so the ring weight varies from 114 px at the thin side to 190 px at the
  bottom. That variation is what stops it reading as a typeface `O`.

## Palette

- Night: `#070E18`
- Signal indigo: `#4153FB` (upper)
- Warm white: `#F7F4EE` (lower)

Theme-native. No theme work.

## Geometry

- Master grid: 1024 × 1024
- Outer: 428 × 688 px ellipse, centred
- Counter: 200 × 352 px, centred at (486, 476)
- Minimum ring weight: 114 px (11.1%) — ~3.2 px at 29 px

## Where this is weak

- It still reads as a letter `O` first. The off-centre counter weakens that read
  but does not defeat it.
- The two-tone split now invites a second wrong read: a progress ring or a
  loading spinner.
- A single closed ring is the most crowded shape in logo design, so trademark
  screening will be uncomfortable.
- Best small-size performance of the three and the thinnest idea of the three.

## Status

Concept for evaluation. Not production.
