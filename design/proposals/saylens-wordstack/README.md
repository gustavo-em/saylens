# SayLens — Wordstack concept

## Idea

Three rounded word bars represent the product loop: **see → name → say**. The
unequal lengths feel like real text lines; the seven-degree rise adds progress
and energy without turning the mark into a face or mascot.

## Palette

- Night: `#070E18`
- Calm indigo: `#5E41D2`
  (was Signal indigo `#4153FB`; deepened and desaturated so the mark sits in
  the same family as the app's accent rather than a neighbouring one)
- Warm white: `#F7F4EE`

## Geometry

- Master grid: 1024 × 1024
- Minimum bar weight: 120 px (11.7% of the master)
- Clear gaps: 48 px (4.7% of the master)
- Motion angle: −7°
- Recommended minimum mark size: 24 px; tested app-icon sizes: 29, 40, 60 px

## Status

The palette here is the one that ships. The geometry is not. The packaged iOS
and Android icons and the in-app mark draw full pills at −38°, taken from
`src/assets/lesingo-mark.svg` and rendered by `scripts/generate-brand-assets.py`;
this sheet records the original −7° concept with its half-capped bars.
Before release: perform trademark similarity screening, finalize/outline the
wordmark typography, and test the icon on physical devices.
