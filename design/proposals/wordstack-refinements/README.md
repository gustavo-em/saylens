# Wordstack refinements

The Wordstack mark was kept for its craft — chunky rounded bars, varied lengths,
one indigo against two creams — and refined only to break the one thing wrong
with it: at a glance it reads as a hamburger menu.

Four things cause that read, and each variant attacks a different one:

1. three roughly parallel bars
2. even gaps between them
3. a horizontal axis
4. three elements of the same kind

Every variant keeps the original bar height (120 px), the fully rounded caps, and
the exact palette, so they are comparable on composition alone.

## Results

**`4-inclinado` — works. Recommended.** Same three bars, angle committed to −38°
instead of the original −7°, scaled 1.25 to fill the square's diagonal. A menu is
horizontal; this is decisively not. It is the smallest change with the largest
effect, it keeps everything that made the original attractive, and it fills the
frame better than the original did. Risk: at a hard glance it can read as an
italic `≡`, and diagonal bar marks are not rare. Worth a cold first-read test.

**`2-medidor` — works. The decisive option.** Rotates the axis to vertical: three
columns of different heights on a shared baseline, reading as a voice level
meter, which is on concept for a speaking app. It kills the menu read completely
and is the cleanest of all six at 29 px. Two costs: audio-bars is itself a
well-used icon and sits close to a bar chart, and going upright loses the tilt
and the energy that came with it.

**`1-leque` — failed.** Fanning the three bars to different angles breaks the
parallelism in theory. In practice it reads as a menu that was badly aligned,
which is the same trap the original's −7° fell into: rotation below the threshold
where it reads as intent just reads as a mistake.

**`3-ponto` — failed.** Replacing the indigo bar with a disc does kill the menu
read, and immediately creates a worse one: a disc above two horizontal bars is a
head above shoulders. It reads as a user avatar.

**`5-desalinhado` — failed.** Uneven gaps (60 and 132 px) and hard horizontal
offsets do break the grid, but the result reads as sloppy rather than composed —
the bottom bar drifts away from the other two and the mark loses its centre.

## Next

Whichever direction is chosen still needs the mono variant, the iOS dark and
tinted appearances, and the Android adaptive foreground and monochrome exports,
plus trademark screening. Nothing here is production.
